Gem Cat Minesweeper Support

Thank you for playing Gem Cat Minesweeper, a cozy, cat-and-gem-themed take on the classic Minesweeper puzzle game.

How to Play

Use the numbered clues to determine which spaces are safe. Reveal safe spaces, place flags where you believe mines are hidden, and clear the board without uncovering a mine.

Troubleshooting

If you experience a problem, try the following:

  • Close and reopen the app.
  • Restart your device.
  • Make sure your device is running the latest available version of iOS.
  • Check the App Store for an available Gem Cat Minesweeper update.

Contact Support

If you still need assistance or would like to report a problem, email: gemcatminesweeper@yahoo.com

When reporting a problem, please include your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of what happened.
